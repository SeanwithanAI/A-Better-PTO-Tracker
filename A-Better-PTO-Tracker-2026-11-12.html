Pick a bank, then tap a day to spend it. Turn on ½ day (or Shift-click) to book a half day; tap again to undo. On a phone, swipe the calendar left or right to change months. Every day shows what each bank will be worth on that date, so you can see what a week off costs you before you book it.
Clear day wipes every bank on one date. Clicking the selected bank again turns it off, and days stop responding.
Green outline is payday, counted from the recent payday you enter under Anchor dates. The gray sliver marks where a pay period starts. Purple is a holiday you get off (pick a schedule or add your own under Holidays below); it’s a label only and never touches the math. Blue bar is an on-call week, which is where comp time is earned. Red means that bank has gone negative.
On January 1, anything above a bank’s year-end carryover limit is lost (Vacation keeps up to 192 hours by default). Personal holiday grants 16 hours every January 1, and whatever you didn’t use the year before is lost. The Vacation incentive adds hours on January 1 for using little sick leave the year before (8 hours for 8 or fewer used, 6 for up to 16, 4 for up to 20, by default).
